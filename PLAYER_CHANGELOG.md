# Player release notes

## 0.21.0 - 2026-11-07

- The pause menu has a roomier layout with Settings, Inventory, and Builder tabs. Resume stays within reach while you browse.
- Settings are grouped into Graphics, World & Time, Tools & HUD, Audio, Physics, and Experimental, so sound, clock, tool, and debris controls are easier to find.
- Navigate menu tabs with the arrow keys and Home/End. Press Esc to return to the world, or use I to jump straight to Inventory.

## 0.20.0 - 2026-11-07

- Sunlight now adds a warm halo and soft glare that changes with the time of day. Terrain, buildings, and clouds can shade the sun and block the glare.
- Adjust Sun Glare and Glare Occlusion in Graphics settings to suit your view.

## 0.19.4 - 2026-11-07

- The sun, terrain lighting, and shadows now move together as the time of day changes.
- Cloud shadows and their optional visual guides stay better aligned with the sun and nearby terrain.

## 0.19.1 - 2026-11-07

- Clouds cast moving shadows across the world during the day. Turn Cloud Shadows on or off in Graphics settings.

## 0.19.0 - 2026-11-07

- Explore a new sky with drifting, three-dimensional cloud banks. Fly below, through, or above them.
- Dawn and dusk bring warmer light, while clearer stars and a shaded moon appear at night.

## 0.18.0 - 2026-07-13

- The inventory now includes a creative catalog alongside a saved Backpack for finite supplies.
