# Player release notes

## 0.25.3 - 2026-11-15

- Gaps in leaves now form uneven, varied patches instead of a repeating grid of plus-shaped holes. Their shapes and shadows stay steady as you move.

## 0.25.2 - 2026-11-15

- Sun shadows now meet the foot of solid walls and chipped ledges without leaving a bright outline on the ground.

## 0.25.1 - 2026-11-15

- Bird formations arrive from the distance, fly across the sky overhead, and leave again. Their old short routes could still look like they were orbiting you.
- You can see the flocks approaching from the ground. Sky birds have clearer silhouettes, while lower birds keep their tree visits.

## 0.25.0 - 2026-11-15

- Leaf blocks now have irregular gaps that let you see through the foliage and let sunlight filter through their shadows.
- Sky and Lamp light can pass through leaves, including damaged leaves. Leaves still support you and can be built with as before.

## 0.24.2 - 2026-11-15

- Chipped ledges now cast sun shadows that follow the pieces left standing. A light hit also keeps the sunlit and shaded sides of a block facing the right way.

## 0.24.1 - 2026-11-15

- Birds build up speed smoothly as they fly, with faster cruising and double their previous top speeds.

## 0.24.0 - 2026-11-08

- The world has a new Sunwashed pixel-art look, with warm earth, olive grass, pale stone, richer foliage, and weathered wood.
- Connected surface patterns and varied grain make broad stretches of terrain feel less repetitive.
- Block previews in the hotbar, Inventory, Backpack, and Builder now show the same textures used in the world.

## 0.23.1 - 2026-11-08

- Birds now travel between their own destinations through the surrounding world. Their old circular routes could make them appear to orbit where you entered the world.

## 0.23.0 - 2026-11-08

- Faster bird formations cruise high near the clouds, with flapping wings, glides, and banking turns.
- Solo birds zip around lower down and can settle on tree canopies before taking flight again.
- Friendly Wanderers are now an optional experiment. Enable them under Pause > Debug when you want to try the three characters; they default off.

## 0.22.0 - 2026-11-08

- Meet friendly wanderers near where you enter a world. They stroll, take breaks, and turn to greet you when you approach.
- Each wanderer has a name and their own look. Come close and press E to say hello.
- Wanderers use the terrain around them, including carved ground, and pause with the game. This first population resets when you leave or change worlds.

## 0.21.1 - 2026-11-07

- The pause menu now has a Debug tab for Core Aim Preview, Health Bars, and Cloud Shadow Guides. F6 still toggles Core Aim Preview while playing.

## 0.21.0 - 2026-11-07

- The pause menu has a roomier layout with Settings, Inventory, and Builder tabs. Resume stays within reach while you browse.
- Settings are grouped into Graphics, World & Time, Tools & HUD, Audio, Physics, and Experimental, so sound, clock, tool, and debris controls are easier to find.
- Navigate menu tabs with the arrow keys and Home/End. Press Esc to return to the world, or use I to jump straight to Inventory.

## 0.20.0 - 2026-11-07

- Sunlight now adds a warm halo and soft glare that changes with the time of day. Terrain, buildings, and clouds can shade the sun and block the glare.
- Adjust Sun Glare and Glare Occlusion in Graphics settings to suit your view.

## 0.19.4 - 2026-11-07

- The sun, terrain lighting, and shadows now move together as the time of day changes.
- Cloud shadows and their optional visual guides stay better aligned with the sun and nearby terrain.

## 0.19.1 - 2026-11-07

- Clouds cast moving shadows across the world during the day. Turn Cloud Shadows on or off in Graphics settings.

## 0.19.0 - 2026-11-07

- Explore a new sky with drifting, three-dimensional cloud banks. Fly below, through, or above them.
- Dawn and dusk bring warmer light, while clearer stars and a shaded moon appear at night.

## 0.18.0 - 2026-07-13

- The inventory now includes a creative catalog alongside a saved Backpack for finite supplies.
